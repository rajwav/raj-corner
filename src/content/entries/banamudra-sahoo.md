### **01 — THE HUMAN BEHIND THE CHAOS 😭**

Banamudra is a **defence enthusiast**, with a natural curiosity for how things work and a particular interest in **mechanical science**. Somehow, after her branch upgrade, she ended up in CSE — a field she probably never imagined she'd be studying. 😭 And the funny part is, while she often says she doesn't understand CSE, she occasionally understands things that people around her struggle to get their heads around. Maybe that's just her way of proving that she doesn't need to follow the usual route.

She can fit into almost any field — **NCC, sports, academics, mechanical science, technology**, and probably several things we haven't discovered yet. She is a **Junior NCC Officer**, was the **Assistant Coordinator of the Sports Society in her second year**, and is now the **Coordinator of Illumina in her third year**. In her second semester, she was also the **university topper** — and somehow, she's now managing all of this while maintaining a solid **9+ SGPA and CGPA**. 😭

I honestly don't know what career she'll eventually choose, and maybe she doesn't either. But whatever she chooses, I'm pretty sure she'll find a way to do something remarkable with it. I hope she reaches the very top of whichever field she chooses, and hopefully, wherever that journey takes her, there'll still be plenty of stories for us to add along the way.

**One person. Many possibilities. And somehow, every one of them looks like it could suit her.** 🌷

### **02 — THE CR CHRONICLES 🫡**

Before CSE-A happened, Banamudra had already been the **CR of Production** in her first year, while I had been the **CR of CSE during first year**. Then she changed her branch to CSE, and becoming CR wasn't really on either of our minds. She wasn't initially interested, and honestly, **I wasn't interested in continuing as CR either after first year.** Somehow, after we talked about it, she agreed to apply — and once she did, I agreed too. And just like that, **Banamudra & Pitambar became the CRs of CSE-A.**

That first day was something else. Both of us showed up in formals, CVs in hand, looking far more official than we probably felt. After everything was decided, we shook hands — and that was the beginning of our little CR era.

Then came classes, responsibilities, seniors, juniors, ragging, random problems, and the usual university chaos. Somewhere along the way, Banamudra developed one particularly useful strategy: whenever she owed me a treat, she'd casually remind me that **“you already have the best co-CR, why do you need a treat?”** Apparently, being her co-CR is now a legally recognized substitute for food. 💀

Then came the day I had some inside information that I might be removed from the CR position. I told her about it, and her response was basically: **“If they remove you, I'll go talk to the teachers — and I'll resign too.”** Maybe she said it casually, maybe she meant every word — either way, that's one sentence that earned its place in the archives. 🌷

Eventually, I was officially removed. But somehow, the title disappeared while the partnership didn't. We still worked together, handled things, discussed class matters, and continued functioning like co-CRs anyway. Later, **HOD Sir asked me to take up CR responsibilities again**, and after some time, **we officially became co-CRs once more.**

And honestly, that's why the combination works. Different personalities, different strengths, plenty of chaos, occasional awkwardness, and somehow a ridiculous amount of coordination.

**Officially: Co-CRs of CSE-A.**\
 **Unofficially: the GOATed CR duo — the best CRs in VSSUT history, according to two completely unbiased sources.** 🫡🏆\
 **Banamudra × Pitambar — one class, two CRs, zero normal days.**
