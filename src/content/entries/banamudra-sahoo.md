This creature has many names. JungleCoin. BM. Roja. Banamudra. And somehow, all of them belong to the same person.
She is a Junior NCC Officer, holding the highest NCC post attained by a girl at VSSUT and one of the highest student leadership positions in the university. In second year, she was the Assistant Coordinator of the Sports Society. Now, in third year, she is the Coordinator of Illumina — the annual sports event of VSSUT.
And somewhere in between all of that, she became our CR.
In first year, she was the CR of Production while I was the CR of CSE. Then second year happened. Her CGPA was high enough for a branch upgrade, and she joined CSE. Somehow, we ended up deciding that she would become our CR — and from there, we became the CRs of CSE-A together.
Banamudra and Pitambar Panda.
Two CRs. One class. Too many responsibilities. Probably too many stories.
We are the best.
And honestly, beneath all the jokes, titles, responsibilities, and everything else that happened during engineering, there is something much simpler.
She is the person who matters most to me in this entire engineering journey.
Maybe years from now, I'll remember the classes, the exams, the projects, the events, and all the chaos differently. But I think I'll always remember this person.
Some people are just part of a chapter. Some people become the reason you remember the chapter at all.
She is one of those people.
