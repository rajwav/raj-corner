I don't know exactly when it happened, but sometime tonight I started thinking about leaving.

Not running away.

Just going somewhere nobody knows my name.

Somewhere I could wake up without knowing what the day is supposed to become.

Maybe a road.

Maybe the ocean.

Maybe a city I've never seen.

---

## The strange thing

I wasn't unhappy.

I was just restless.

There is a difference.

Sometimes you can be completely fine and still feel like there is another version of your life waiting somewhere.

That thought scares me a little.

It also makes me smile.

> Maybe curiosity is just homesickness for a place you've never been.

I keep thinking about the roads I've saved, the cars I want to drive, the places I want to see, and all the tiny things I haven't experienced yet.

Maybe that's why I keep collecting these little memories.

They are breadcrumbs.

---

### Things I thought about tonight

- Driving somewhere without planning the route
- Watching the sunrise from a beach
- Learning something difficult just because it looks impossible
- Building something that actually works
- Taking a photograph I will still like ten years from now
- Sitting somewhere completely unfamiliar and doing absolutely nothing
