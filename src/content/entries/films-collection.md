### Selected Films

- **Christopher Nolan Films**: Non-linear narrative puzzles, practical scale, and grand philosophical stakes (Inception, Interstellar, Oppenheimer, The Prestige, The Dark Knight).
- **John Wick**: Kinetic gun-fu, underworld lore, and legendary stunt discipline.
- **12th Fail**: Manoj Kumar Sharma's relentless perseverance against impossible socio-economic odds—raw authenticity and heart.
- **Sitaare Zameen Par**: Empathy, perspective shifts, and stories with enduring warmth.
- **Taqdeer**: High emotional resonance and memorable cinematic rhythm.
- **Spider-Man**: Relatable moral responsibility and grounded heroism.
- **Fight Club**: David Fincher's biting critique of consumer sedation, identity fracture, and raw physical awakening.
