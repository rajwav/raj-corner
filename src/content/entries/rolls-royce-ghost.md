### Why I Like It

The Ghost approaches motoring from the opposite direction of raw speed—it treats isolation, silence, and effortlessness as the highest virtues. The Planar suspension system monitors road topology in real-time, the doors are electrically operated with whisper-quiet motors, and the V12 provides seamless, uninterrupted surge without ever raising its voice.

It doesn't scream for attention like loud supercars; its presence commands the room simply by standing still.
