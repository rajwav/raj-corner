### The Scale of the Insane

A single teaspoon of neutron star material would weigh approximately six billion tons on Earth. Magnetars carry magnetic fields so violent they could dissolve atoms at thousands of kilometers away. 

And yet, all matter we can observe—every star, planet, galaxy, and speck of dust—accounts for only roughly five percent of the cosmos. The rest is Dark Matter and Dark Energy, phenomena we can measure through gravitational scaffolding but cannot yet touch or understand.
