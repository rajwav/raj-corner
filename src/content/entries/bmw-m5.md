### Why I Like It

The M5 represents the pinnacle of having your cake and eating it too. You can drive three colleagues across town in leather-clad executive luxury, and ten seconds later flick the red M2 button on the steering wheel to unleash terrifying supercar-shaming pace.

It is understated enough to park outside a boardroom without drawing vulgar stares, yet engineered with the raw heart of a motorsport machine.
