### Beyond Our Solar Bubble

We grew up with nine planets (or eight), but the thousands of exoplanets cataloged by Kepler and James Webb reveal planetary systems that defy every familiar expectation.

There are hot Jupiters tidally locked to their host stars with temperatures hot enough to vaporize iron; ocean worlds hundreds of kilometers deep where exotic high-pressure ice exists at boiling temperatures; and rogue planets ejected from their parent systems, drifting forever through cold interstellar space in pitch blackness.
