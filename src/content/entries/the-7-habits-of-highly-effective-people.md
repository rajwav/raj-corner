### Why It Matters

Covey's distinction between the "Personality Ethic" (quick fixes, communication tricks, surface charm) and the "Character Ethic" (integrity, humility, fidelity, temperance) is the core strength of this book.

Focusing on the circle of influence rather than the circle of concern—taking proactive ownership over what you can actually control—is a quiet discipline that changes how daily friction is handled.
