I had always been scared of injections, so when the blood-donation camp came up, I wasn't exactly volunteering with confidence. 😭 Banamudra happened to tell me about the camp, something she knew about through NCC and the organizations involved in conducting it. She casually mentioned that I could donate if I wanted. Somehow, that small piece of information turned into a decision to finally face that fear.

We initially planned to donate in the morning, but the timing didn't work out. With the morning plan cancelled, the day eventually brought us back to the camp in the evening.

After 5 PM, **Banamudra, Bharti, Satyam, Swati and I** met at the donation camp. We registered, filled in our details, and went through the initial checks. The weighing gave us **67.8 kg for me and 51.8 kg for Banamudra**.\
 My blood group was **B+**, while Banamudra's was **O+**.\
 Swati didn't donate herself, but became our official **moral-support department and photographer for the evening**, making sure nobody escaped without photographic evidence. 😂

**Satyam went first, followed by me, Bharti and Banamudra.** At one point, all four of us were donating at the same time, while Swati was busy capturing individual videos of everyone.

**banana, cake, smoothies, chocolate and refreshments.** 😂

Once everything was done, we collected our **blood-donation certificates** and took another group photo before heading back.

By the end of the day, something that had started with me being genuinely nervous about a needle had turned into one of those unexpectedly memorable evenings.

I didn't just donate blood that day.

**I faced something I was afraid of — and somehow, it felt much easier with good people around. ❤️**
