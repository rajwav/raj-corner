### Long-Form Storytelling

- **Breaking Bad**: The tragic, inevitable transformation of Walter White into Heisenberg. Tightest plotting in modern TV.
- **Money Heist (La Casa de Papel)**: The Professor's contingency planning and heist psychology.
- **Stranger Things**: 80s synth atmosphere, suburban camaraderie, and Lovecraftian dread.
- **Alice in Borderland**: Extreme game theory, survival puzzles, and psychological pressure.
- **Game of Thrones**: Political maneuvering, dynastic betrayals, and grand fantasy scale.
